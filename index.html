To · Srishti · My Sister

There are people who light up a room, and then there is you — who somehow manages to light up the room AND finish all the snacks before anyone else even knew there were snacks. A gift, truly.

I have watched you grow from a tiny creature who cried if I so much as looked at the TV remote, into a slightly taller creature who still cries if I look at the TV remote. The evolution is breathtaking. Somewhere along the way you became genuinely funny, annoyingly smart, and so embarrassingly talented that I am legally obligated to be proud of you.

On this birthday, I want you to know: everything great you think about yourself? 100% accurate. Every complaint I've ever had about you? Also accurate — but today I'll keep that part to myself. May this year give you everything you deserve, may your WiFi always be fast, and may you never have to share your birthday cake. Happy Birthday. 🎂

— Ravi
